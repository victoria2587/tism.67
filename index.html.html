A personalized sensory companion

Comfort, made personal.

Awe-TiSm is a concept for a customizable physical companion for autistic individuals. It is being explored around each person's sensory preferences, comfort and interaction style.

Learn moreExplore the concept
☁️
Different people. Different preferences.

Designed around individual preferences

No two people experience the world in exactly the same way. Our idea is to explore whether a physical companion that can be personalized may offer an additional source of comfort for some individuals.

Explore possible interactions

The concept may include adjustable sensory features. Every feature would be optional and tailored to the user's preferences.

🤲

Touch

Consider different textures and gentle touch interactions.

💡

Light

Explore soft, adjustable lighting that can be switched off.

🔈

Sound

Optional sounds with adjustable volume and a quiet mode.

〰️

Vibration

Optional gentle vibration, with control over intensity.

🧸

Personal style

Explore forms, colors and finishes that users prefer.

⚙️

Careful customization

Caregivers may be able to help configure settings with the user.

Support, not a substitute.

Awe-TiSm is intended as a possible additional comfort tool—not a replacement for parents, caregivers, therapists, healthcare professionals or human relationships. The concept is still being researched and validated.
